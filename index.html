The Farming Cycle

Our Five Steps of Responsible Agriculture

From the first summer furrow to the final storage bin, we manage every stage with attention to detail, soil health, and natural balance.

Step 01

1. Land Preparation & Soil Tilling

Healthy crops begin with healthy soil. Before every sowing cycle, we carry out deep summer plowing to expose the soil to natural sunlight, eliminating dormant weeds and harmful larvae. We incorporate decomposed farmyard manure and organic compost to enrich humus levels, followed by precision laser-leveling to ensure uniform moisture distribution across the entire acreage.

  • Deep summer plowing
  • Organic manure enrichment
  • Laser leveling for water savings
Step 02

2. Seed Selection & Precision Sowing

We select clean, certified, non-chemically treated seeds adapted to our regional climate. Seeds are treated with biological inoculants (like Rhizobium for pulses and Azotobacter for grains) to bolster natural root vigor. We utilize calibrated seed drills to place seeds at uniform depth and row-to-row spacing, ensuring optimal germination rates and seedling aeration.

  • Climate-hardy seed varieties
  • Bio-inoculant seed treatment
  • Uniform seed drill spacing
Step 03

3. Conscious Irrigation & Water Management

Water is our most precious resource. We employ planned irrigation tailored to critical growth intervals (such as crown rooting, flowering, and grain filling) rather than flooding indiscriminately. By combining lined water distribution furrows, rainwater retention bunds, and micro-irrigation channels, we avoid waterlogging and conserve ground water reserves.

  • Critical stage scheduling
  • Field bund water retention
  • Zero unnecessary runoff
Step 04

4. Natural Crop Care & Plant Nourishment

We spend daily hours walking our crop rows. Routine physical weeding and inter-cultivation aerate the upper root zones while removing nutrient competition. Rather than blanket spraying, we practice Integrated Pest Monitoringâ€”encouraging natural predatory insects (ladybirds and spiders) and applying neem-based bio-sprays only when strictly necessary to safeguard the crop.

  • Manual inter-row weeding
  • Neem oil natural protection
  • Daily in-field health scouting
Step 05

5. Timely Harvesting & Proper Storage

Timing is everything during harvest. We monitor grain moisture levels closely, waiting until crops achieve peak physiological maturity under natural sunlight. Harvested grain is threshed carefully to avoid kernel breakage, sun-dried on clean yards, and stored in dry, well-ventilated granaries with natural neem leaves to protect against moisture and storage pests.

  • Harvesting at optimal grain dryness
  • Natural open-air sun curing
  • Moisture-safe grain storage
